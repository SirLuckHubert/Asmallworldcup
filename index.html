How to play

Three things to learn

Drag

Press near your player and pull. The arrow shows where you'll fly and how hard.

Release

Let go and you're thrown. You keep that momentum — throw again in the air to change direction.

Score

Meet the ball while you're moving fast. The faster you're going, the harder it goes.

  • Mouse, trackpad or touch — on an iPad just drag on the pitch.
  • No keyboard? The pause button sits next to the clock.
  • Progress saves by itself; the dot above the game turns green when it has.
