How it works

  1. Drag. Press near your player and pull back. The line shows where you'll go and how hard.
  2. Let go. You get thrown and you keep that momentum. Throw again in the air to change direction.
  3. Hit it moving. The faster you're going when you meet the ball, the harder it goes.

Mouse, trackpad or touch. On an iPad just drag on the pitch.
