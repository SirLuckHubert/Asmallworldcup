What is it

Football, if the players were made of jelly

Two little ragdolls, one ball, two goals and a lot of gravity. Every touch comes from momentum — you throw yourself at the ball and hope your head gets there first.

Win matches to earn coins, open packs, build a squad of 200+ original cards and take them through a World Cup, a club season, or a quick game against a mate's favourite player.

Real physics, no animations

Nothing is scripted. Every goal comes out of the collision, the spin and the bounce.

Cards worth chasing

Packs, a Star Signing store and abilities that actually change how your player throws.

Gameplay

Everything is built from the physics up

One mechanic, endless chaos. These are the parts that make it stick.

Fling to move

Drag from your player and let go. Longer drag, harder throw — in the air too.

Trailing ragdoll

Head, arms and legs swing against your movement, then settle when you land.

A ball with weight

It bounces off posts, spins, curves and carries whatever pace you hit it with.

Packs and pulls

Walkout animations, pool odds, pity counters and a store that's actually worth saving for.

30 abilities

Bronze to Diamond. Cannon, Anchor, Poacher, Silky — they change the feel, not just a number.

Seasons that matter

Seven knockout rounds, a twelve-game club league, stamina, XP and a table that updates.

How to play

Three things to learn

Drag

Press anywhere near your player and pull. The arrow shows where you'll fly and how hard.

Release

Let go and you're thrown. You keep that momentum — throw again mid-air to change direction.

Score

Meet the ball while you're moving fast. The faster you're going, the further it goes.

Good to know

  • Mouse, trackpad or touch — iPads work, just drag on the pitch.
  • The pause button sits next to the clock if you have no keyboard.
  • Progress saves by itself; the dot above the game turns green when it has.
  • Fullscreen makes the pitch much easier to read on a small screen.
